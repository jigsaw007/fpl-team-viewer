About FPL Peek

FPL Peek is a Fantasy Premier League planning and analysis workspace built to make common FPL decisions easier to review in one place.

What FPL Peek does

FPL Peek brings together tools for team analysis, transfer comparisons, fixture planning, player research, captain decisions, price monitoring, live gameweek information and multi-gameweek planning.

The Planner is a simulation tool. Changes made inside FPL Peek do not change a user's Fantasy Premier League team. Managers remain responsible for making any real transfers, captain changes or chip selections on the official Fantasy Premier League website.

How the app works

Most public football and FPL information shown in the app is obtained from Fantasy Premier League data made available through its public API. FPL Peek organizes that information and adds its own calculations, comparisons and planning views.

Users can use the Planner without creating an account. Local plans are saved in the browser. An optional FPL Peek account allows supported planner data to be synchronized across devices.

What FPL Peek does not do

  • FPL Peek does not ask for a Fantasy Premier League password.
  • FPL Peek does not sign in to a user's official Fantasy Premier League account.
  • FPL Peek does not submit transfers, captain changes, chips or other team changes to Fantasy Premier League.
  • FPL Peek does not guarantee future points, price changes, player availability or match outcomes.

Independence

FPL Peek is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Premier League. Fantasy Premier League, Premier League club names and related marks belong to their respective owners.
