About

FPL decisions are easier when the context is in one place.

FPL Peek is an independent Fantasy Premier League planning and analysis workspace built around a simple idea: show the information behind a decision, not just a recommendation.

Updated 2 September 2026About FPL Peek

Why FPL Peek exists

Playing FPL usually means jumping between a squad page, fixtures, transfer trends, player statistics, captain discussions and mini-league standings. FPL Peek brings those workflows together so a manager can move from “what happened?” to “what should I investigate next?” without losing the context.

The project focuses on practical decisions: whether a transfer is urgent, how fixtures change over several Gameweeks, who offers captaincy upside, which players are moving rapidly in the market, and why a mini-league gap changed.

What you can do here

My Team and Home use a public FPL Team ID to show squad and Gameweek context. Planner and Team Builder let you simulate future moves. Players, Scout and Player Compare help create shortlists. Fixtures, Captain Picks, Transfer Stats, Price Watch, DEFCON Tracker and Set Pieces add specialised context. League Analyzer and Manager Compare help explain performance relative to other managers.

FPL Peek also publishes guides because a number without explanation is easy to misuse. The Guides & Analysis library explains the scoring rules, transfer process, captaincy, chips and how market data should be interpreted.

Data and independence

Most public FPL information displayed by the app is obtained from Fantasy Premier League data made available publicly. FPL Peek then organises the information and, in some tools, applies calculations or heuristics. Where a value is predictive rather than official, it should be treated as an estimate.

FPL Peek is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Premier League. Fantasy Premier League, Premier League club names and related marks belong to their respective owners.

Your official FPL account stays separate

FPL Peek does not ask for your Fantasy Premier League password, does not sign in to your official account and does not submit transfers, captain changes or chips. Planner actions are simulations. You make real team changes on the official FPL website.

Transparency

Predictions are not guarantees. Player availability, team selection, match events and the underlying FPL systems can change. We publish a methodology page so visitors can see the difference between official data and FPL Peek interpretation.

Corrections and contact

If a tool appears inconsistent with official data, we want to know. Include the page, Gameweek and relevant player or Team ID when possible. Visit the contact page or email account@fplpeek.com.
