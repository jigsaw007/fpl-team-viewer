Transfer strategy

Free Transfers and Points Hits: When Patience Beats Activity

A framework for rolling transfers, taking hits and valuing flexibility across multiple Gameweeks.

Updated 2 September 20268 min readBy FPL Peek
Decision principle: Use the metric to understand a trade-off. No single statistic can guarantee future FPL points.

A free transfer has option value

A transfer you do not use immediately can increase your flexibility at a later deadline. That flexibility becomes especially valuable when new injuries, fixture changes or role information appear.

Activity has no bonus points of its own. If your current XI is healthy and well set, keeping the transfer can be the strongest move.

Why rolling can be powerful

Multiple free transfers let you change structure without paying points. They can also allow a two-step move that would be impossible with one isolated transfer.

Rolling is most useful when there is no urgent problem and the next deadline is likely to provide more information.

How to evaluate a hit

A points hit should be compared with the expected difference between the outgoing and incoming players over the period you plan to own them. Do not judge it only by whether the new player outscores the old one in the first match.

Minutes, captaincy and future transfers can all change the calculation.

Hits for injuries versus marginal upgrades

Replacing a player expected to miss several matches can have a clear route to repaying a hit. Paying points to move between two healthy, similarly projected starters is much harder to justify.

The stronger the bench cover, the less urgent an injury transfer may become.

Think two deadlines ahead

Before confirming, sketch the next likely transfer. If today’s move blocks that route, its hidden cost may be larger than the immediate points difference.

This is exactly the type of problem a multi-Gameweek planner can expose before you commit.

Put it into practice

Use the relevant FPL Peek tool to test the idea against your own squad, then make any real changes on the official Fantasy Premier League site.
