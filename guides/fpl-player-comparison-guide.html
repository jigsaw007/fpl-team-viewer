How to Compare Two FPL Players Properly

A practical comparison framework covering minutes, role, fixtures, price, routes to points and squad fit.

Updated 2 September 20268 min readBy FPL Peek
Decision principle: Use the metric to understand a trade-off. No single statistic can guarantee future FPL points.
On this pageBegin with minutesCompare routes to pointsUse fixtures as contextPrice includes opportunity costThe best player is squad-dependent

Begin with minutes

If one player is substantially more secure for starts and 70-plus minutes, that can outweigh a small advantage elsewhere. Start probability and expected minutes form the base of every other comparison.

Do not use per-90 statistics without checking whether the player is actually likely to receive 90 minutes.

Compare routes to points

List how each player can score: goals, assists, clean sheets, saves, defensive contributions, set pieces, penalties and bonus. Players with several independent routes often have a more reliable floor.

The weighting depends on position and role, so avoid comparing raw attacking numbers without context.

Use fixtures as context

Compare the next several fixtures rather than only the next opponent. A slightly weaker player can be a better transfer if his run is meaningfully stronger and his minutes are safer.

FDR is useful for scanning, but role and team strength still decide whether the fixture can be exploited.

Price includes opportunity cost

A £1.5m saving is not abstract. Ask what that money upgrades elsewhere. The cheaper player can be the better squad decision even if his individual projection is slightly lower.

This is why Player Compare should be followed by a look at your full team structure.

The best player is squad-dependent

Your captaincy, bench, free transfers and future plan all matter. Two managers can rationally choose different players from the same comparison.

The purpose of comparison is to expose the trade-offs clearly enough that the squad context can decide.

Put it into practice

Use the relevant FPL Peek tool to test the idea against your own squad, then make any real changes on the official Fantasy Premier League site.
