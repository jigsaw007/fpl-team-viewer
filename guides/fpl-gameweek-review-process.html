Review process

How to Review an FPL Gameweek Without Hindsight Bias

Turn a finished Gameweek into useful information by separating decision quality from the points outcome.

Updated 2 September 20268 min readBy FPL Peek
Decision principle: Use the metric to understand a trade-off. No single statistic can guarantee future FPL points.
On this pageOutcome is not decision qualityReview captaincy separatelyCheck bench points in contextSeparate bad luck from bad assumptionsWrite one lesson for next week

Outcome is not decision quality

A player can miss a penalty after being an excellent pick, while a weak punt can score from one unlikely shot. Reviewing only the final points teaches the wrong lesson.

Reconstruct what you knew before the deadline and judge whether the assumptions were reasonable.

Review captaincy separately

Because captaincy doubles the score, it deserves its own review. Check expected minutes, role and fixture at the deadline rather than comparing only the winning captain after the fact.

A correct process will still produce blanks.

Check bench points in context

A high bench score is painful, but sometimes both starting choices were sensible. Look at the information available before deciding that the benching decision was wrong.

Repeated bench hauls may instead reveal too much budget spread across players you consider equally startable.

Separate bad luck from bad assumptions

Bad luck is a good process producing an unfavourable outcome. A bad assumption is believing a player was nailed when clear rotation evidence existed. The second category is actionable.

Write down which assumption failed so the next decision can improve.

Write one lesson for next week

Do not rebuild your entire strategy after every Gameweek. Record one or two concrete lessons, then move forward.

This keeps review productive and limits emotional transfers immediately after matches.

Put it into practice

Use the relevant FPL Peek tool to test the idea against your own squad, then make any real changes on the official Fantasy Premier League site.
