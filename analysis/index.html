How we analyse

A practical FPL decision process.

Each Gameweek page combines a clear decision framework with relevant FPL Peek tools and current data. The aim is to help you understand the trade-offs behind captaincy, transfers, fixtures and differentials before making a move.

1 · IdentifyFind the real squad problem

Start with injuries, minutes risk, structure or captaincy rather than chasing the previous score.

2 · CompareUse data with context

Fixtures, form, ownership and transfers are most useful when they support a clear football case.

3 · DecideChoose the move that fits

The best option is the one that improves your own squad, not simply the most popular name.
