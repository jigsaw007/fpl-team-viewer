How it works

A fresh squad for each Gameweek

FPL Peek uses public FPL data to build a squad for the selected round.

Strict FPL rules

Exactly 2 goalkeepers, 5 defenders, 5 midfielders and 3 forwards, within £100.0m and no more than 3 players from one club.

Starting XI + bench

The strongest valid formation becomes the XI. The four remaining players form an ordered bench, including a reserve goalkeeper.

Captain & vice

The captain is selected from the starting XI. If the captain does not play, the vice-captain takes the double-points role.

Gameweek score

After the round, the page applies official player points, captaincy and valid autosubs to show how FPL Peek performed.

How the Team Builder works

01

Squad rules

A valid squad is 15 players within a £100.0m budget, with at most 3 players from any one club. You must pick exactly:

  • GK 2 goalkeepers
  • DEF 5 defenders
  • MID 5 midfielders
  • FWD 3 forwards
02

How the score is built

Your full squad is still 15 players, but the Builder shows the suggested 11 starters on the pitch and the other 4 on the bench. For each Gameweek in the selected 1, 3 or 5-GW window, FPL Peek chooses the strongest legal XI and captain.

If you activate Triple Captain or Bench Boost, the chip changes the projected score for the first Gameweek in that window. Wildcard and Free Hit are shown as planning chips but do not add artificial points.

03

Grades

The squad is compared with a stronger optimized benchmark. Small projection gaps now matter more, so top grades are deliberately harder to earn:

S A B C D E F

S is near-optimal; F means lots of room to improve. The grade moves as you swap players in and out.

04

An honest note on the numbers

The per-player estimate is our own transparent formula — recent form, how easy the upcoming fixtures are, and whether the player is fit and starting. It is not a trained prediction model, so treat the score as a smart guide for comparing squads, not a guaranteed forecast.

05

How AutoPick works

AutoPick builds a legal 15-player squad for the selected 1, 3 or 5-GW window. It looks at FPL Peek projections, upcoming fixtures, form, points-per-game, availability and value while respecting the £100.0m budget, required positions and the 3-player-per-club limit.

If you already picked some players, AutoPick tries to keep those choices and fill the remaining slots. Once the squad is complete, the Builder automatically chooses the suggested XI, bench and captain. You can change every AutoPick choice afterwards.

AutoPick is a planning suggestion, not a guarantee of future points. The active chip is reflected in the projected squad score.

06

How Optimize works

Optimize squad is for a team you already built and want to improve. It keeps the same legal 15-player structure, searches for meaningful same-position upgrades, and respects your budget, club limit, selected 1/3/5-GW window and active chip.

It deliberately ignores tiny sideways moves. Before anything changes, you get a before-and-after review showing the projected gain, grade change and every suggested OUT → IN swap. You choose whether to apply them.

AutoPick can rebuild the squad. Optimize is the lighter-touch option for improving your existing choices.

Understanding the signal

Progress shows movement towards the next possible price change. A value at or above 100% is treated as Very Likely, but it still does not guarantee a change because transfer activity can move before the 00:00 UK deadline. Where official predictor progress is not available in FPL Peek's data feed, the page falls back to a transparent estimate based on net transfers, ownership and player availability.
