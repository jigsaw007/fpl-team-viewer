Contact

Contact FPL Peek

Questions, bug reports and corrections are welcome. The most useful reports include enough detail for us to reproduce what you saw.

Product and data feedback

Email account@fplpeek.com. For a data issue, include the page or tool, Gameweek, player or FPL Team ID involved, and a short description of the difference you noticed.

Account or privacy requests

Use the same email address for account assistance, data or privacy enquiries. Please do not send your Fantasy Premier League password; FPL Peek does not need it.

About the project

FPL Peek is an independent Fantasy Premier League planning and analysis project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Premier League. Public FPL data is organised into tools intended to make comparison and planning easier.

For more detail about calculations and predictions, read the methodology page.

Before emailing

Check the latest Gameweek has finished processing if a live score looks temporary. Official data can settle after matches.
