Behavior after GW3
- Preview / captaincy / transfer / differential / fixture pages move to the active Gameweek if one is current.
- If no Gameweek is active but the next one is known, they move to the upcoming Gameweek.
- Review page points to the latest finished Gameweek context.

AdSense alignment
- Pages include substantial original editorial copy.
- Data widgets are supportive context, not the entire page.
- Methodology / source transparency is preserved.
- Content avoids thin auto-generated placeholder behavior.
